Raw singles

Near Mint or it doesn't get listed

Every raw Pokémon single is checked under 10× for edge whitening, print lines and centering. We photograph front and back — what you see in the listing is the card that ships, not a stock scan.

Vintage

Trimming checks on anything pre-2003

WOTC-era cards get measured and weighed before listing. If a Base Set holo has been trimmed or recolored, it goes in the bin — we'd rather eat the cost than pass it on.

Japanese print

Why JP cards grade better

Japanese print runs tend to have tighter centering and cleaner edges, which is why they gem at a higher rate. We import weekly and list them alongside the English print so you can compare.
