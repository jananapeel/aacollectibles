Leaders

Every deck starts with one

Leader cards sit face-up from turn one and set your colour identity and life total, so they're the first thing most people buy. We stock the base printing and the parallel alt art for each.

Manga rares

The black-and-white chase

Manga rares reproduce a panel straight from the original Oda artwork in black and white. Roughly one per case, which is why an OP-09 Shanks moves for what it does.

Reprints

We tell you which print you're getting

Bandai reprints heavily and the value gap between a first print and a later run is real. Every listing states the print run, so you're never guessing from the photo alone.
