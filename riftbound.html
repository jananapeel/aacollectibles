New to it?

It's a board-control game, not a race

You win by holding Battlefields rather than burning the other player down. That makes Battlefields and cheap units matter more than the splashy legendary everyone opens for — worth knowing before you spend.

Legends

Your champion sits out front

A Legend card fronts your deck and sets which of the six domains you can play, the same way a One Piece leader does. If you're building from scratch, buy the Legend first and the rest around it.

Buying in

Start with a starter, not a box

The two-player Proving Grounds set is the cheapest way in and plays straight out of the wrapper. We'll take it back as credit against singles if you decide to build properly.
