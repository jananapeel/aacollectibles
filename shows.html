Before you come

Ask us to bring something

Anything in the online case can come to the table. Email the listing by 6pm Thursday and we will set it aside — no deposit, and no obligation to buy once you have seen it in hand.

At the table

We buy as well as sell

Bring binders, bulk, or a single high-end card. We quote at the table and pay cash on the spot, or 20% more if you take store credit. Worth emailing ahead for anything large.

What travels

About four binders worth

Pokémon and One Piece singles, the graded slabs, and whatever sealed fits in the car. Riftbound comes to the bigger shows only — ask ahead if you want something specific.
