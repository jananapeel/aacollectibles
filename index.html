From intake to mailbox

How a card gets to you

The same four steps for a $3 common and a four-figure slab.

01

Loupe check

Every raw single goes under 10× magnification — edge whitening, print lines, surface scratches, centering front and back. Anything that won't grade Near Mint doesn't make it to the site.

02

Condition call & photos

Graded on the standard scale — NM, LP, MP — then shot front and back on a neutral card. No stock images, ever: the card in the listing photo is the exact card that ships.

03

Packed like it's ours

Penny sleeve, toploader, team bag, then a rigid mailer. Slabs get bubble-wrapped in a box. Orders over $200 ship signature-required at no extra charge.

04

Out the same day

Orders placed before 3:00 PM ship that afternoon, Monday to Friday. Tracking hits your inbox the moment the label scans.
